OUR HISTORY

From Cape Town to beyond.

The first Woolworths store opened on Adderley Street in Cape Town in 1931, founded by Max Sonnenberg.

The business expanded to Durban, Port Elizabeth and Johannesburg in its early years.

Over time, Woolworths developed a reputation for quality, innovation and value.

Today, Woolworths Holdings Limited has two major operating divisions: Woolworths South Africa and Country Road Group.
